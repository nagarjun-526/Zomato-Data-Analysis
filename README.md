# 🍽️ Zomato Restaurant Data Analysis using Python

## 📌 Project Overview

The restaurant industry generates massive amounts of customer and operational data. Analyzing this information helps businesses understand customer preferences, optimize pricing strategies, improve service quality, and increase customer satisfaction.

This project performs **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on the Zomato restaurant dataset using Python to uncover meaningful insights about:

- Restaurant popularity
- Customer voting behavior
- Online ordering trends
- Rating distributions
- Dining cost preferences
- Restaurant type performance

The analysis demonstrates how data-driven insights can support strategic decision-making in the food and hospitality industry.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

This project aims to answer the following business questions:

### 1️⃣ Do more restaurants offer online ordering or offline services?

Understanding digital adoption among restaurants helps identify opportunities for growth and customer convenience.

### 2️⃣ Which restaurant categories are most popular?

Identifying customer-favored restaurant types helps businesses understand market demand.

### 3️⃣ What is the preferred dining budget for couples?

Discovering spending patterns helps restaurants optimize pricing strategies.

### 4️⃣ How do ratings differ between online and offline restaurants?

Evaluating customer satisfaction across service models helps identify service quality trends.

### 5️⃣ Which restaurant types receive the highest engagement?

Customer votes can be used as a proxy for popularity and public interest.

# 🔎 Key Findings

### Customer Behavior

- Dining restaurants are the most popular category.
- Most customer ratings lie between 3.5 and 4.
- Couples prefer restaurants costing around ₹300.

### Ordering Trends

- Offline ordering remains dominant.
- Cafes attract more online orders than traditional dining restaurants.

### Customer Satisfaction

- Online-order-enabled restaurants receive higher ratings.
- Convenience significantly influences customer perception.
